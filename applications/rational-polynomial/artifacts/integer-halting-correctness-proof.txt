Natural-witness halting polynomial: correctness and artifact binding
3 November 2026

Theorem and scope

Let H be the integer-coefficient polynomial specified by
universal-integer-polynomial.json, SHA256
27a840c06c68bfae88da84a934d6e7f0ca2778dd3e56ec2bf3b4d4eb8171fe04.
For ordinary nonnegative integers e,n,

  exists v in N^1305802: H(e,n,v)=0
  iff the program numbered e halts on input n,

with the numbering in universal-interpreter-spec.txt. Here N includes
zero. The polynomial's hierarchical calls mean literal substitution,
not oracle calls. This proof uses the established alpha/exponentiation
results below. It does not use the new pointwise-converse preprint.

This is a written mathematical and source-correspondence proof. It is
not an Isabelle verification of the JavaScript implementation. Replacing
N by Q in the displayed theorem is not justified. The rational model,
accuracy program, and rational translation require their own proofs.

For b>3, alpha_b(0)=0, alpha_b(1)=1, and
alpha_b(k+2)=b alpha_b(k+1)-alpha_b(k).
The eight-variable system in Builder.alpha is exactly the fifteen
conditions of Exp_Matrices.alpha_equations, with a=X and c=N:
the b bound; three Pell-type equations; r<s; u^2 dividing s;
v+2r=bs; the two congruences specifying w; w>2; the three size
bounds involving X,N; and the two congruences specifying x.
Equation order and reversal of equality do not alter this system.

Builder.lt(A,B) replaces A<B by B=A+1+d, d natural. Builder.divides
replaces A dividing B by B=A*d. Builder.congruent(A,M,B) replaces
equality of remainders by A+M*u=B+M*v. For M>0 this follows by
separating the positive and negative parts of (B-A)/M; for M=0 both
conditions mean A=B. Thus these replacements have both implications.
All their witnesses are fresh. The alpha macro consequently has a
natural witness exactly when b>3 and X=alpha_b(N).

In the positive-base branch of Builder.power, the three alpha calls
set a0=alpha_(Q+4)(R+1), a1=alpha_b(R), a2=alpha_b(R+1).
The remaining equations are exactly exp_equations. The additional
alpha hypothesis b>3 follows from Q>=1, a0>=1 and
b=a0+Q^2+2>=4. The other two branches implement Q=R=0, P=1 and
Q=0<R, P=0. Hence power(P,Q,R) has witnesses exactly when P=Q^R,
including 0^0=1. No natural-subtraction convention is left implicit:
the equations use sums on both sides.

2. Binomial digits and bit operations

For natural N,K put B=2^(N+1). Every binomial coefficient in
(1+B)^N is less than B, since each is at most 2^N. The binomial
theorem therefore gives its base-B digits without carries. Requiring

  (1+B)^N=B^K*(B*u+C)+r,  C<B,  r<B^K

selects exactly the K-th digit C, including C=0 for K>N. The power
macro proves the three power equalities used in this formula. This
establishes Builder.binomial in both directions, also for N=K=0.

Lucas's theorem modulo two gives that binomial(B,A) is odd precisely
when all set bits of A occur in B. Also binomial(A+B,A) is odd
precisely when A and B have disjoint set bits: disjointness makes
addition carry-free; conversely the least position with a shared bit
would have no incoming carry and would erase a set bit of A in A+B.
These are exactly Builder.mask and Builder.orthogonal.

The equations X=Z+A and Y=Z+B, together with pairwise disjointness
of Z,A,B, express Z=X AND Y. The first two disjointness conditions
exclude carries, and the third excludes any further common bit.
Conversely take Z=X AND Y, A=X-Z, B=Y-Z. This proves Builder.bitAnd.

3. Packed-machine theorem for any fixed finite program

Fix a finite add/sub/final-halt program with m states and r registers,
and k<=r natural input values. The first k registers receive those
values and the others receive zero. Acceptance means reaching the
unique final halt with all registers zero.

The compiler quantifies q,c,K,B,T,F,D and imposes
c>0, K=2^c, B=4K>m, T=B^q, and input_i<K. Its equations give
F=(B*T-1)/(B-1)=sum_(t=0)^q B^t and D=(K-1)F. Division here is
only a proof calculation; the emitted equations are BF+1=F+BT
and D+F=KF.

Since B is a power of two, R_j masked by D has exactly q+1 slots
whose values lie in [0,K). The bound R_j<T forces its last slot
to vanish and excludes any higher slot. Each S_i masked by F has
only zero-or-one slots. Since m<B, their sum cannot carry. The
equation sum S_i=F selects exactly one state per time slot, and
S_halt=T places the first and only halt at time q.

In each slot R_j+D has value r_j(t)+K-1<2K<B. Its K-bit is one
exactly when r_j(t)>0. Thus the equation involving
(R_j+D) AND (KF)=K*N_j determines the nonzero indicator. For a
sub instruction V_i=S_i AND N_j and S_i=V_i+W_i determine the
two mutually exclusive branch indicators. There is no subtraction
ambiguity because these are ordinary equalities of natural numbers.

Let A_j,C_j be the packed increment/decrement indicators and I_d
the packed indicators of transitions entering state d. At each time
there is at most one actual instruction and one actual outgoing
branch. Consequently all these indicators have slots zero or one,
even if two branches in the program have the same target.

The compiler emits

  R_j+B*C_j=initial_j+B*R_j+B*A_j,
  S_d=1_(d=0)+B*I_d.

Every register coefficient on either side is at most K, hence less
than B. Every state coefficient is at most one. Uniqueness of base-B
expansions now proves the initial conditions and each exact transition.
The possible extra slot q+1 is zero because the terminal registers
vanish and halt contributes no outgoing operation. Thus every natural
solution decodes to an accepting run. If q=0, these same equations
require that state zero itself is the final halt and all inputs vanish.

Conversely an accepting run has finite length q and finitely many
register values. Choose c so that K exceeds all those values and
B>m; pack the run as above. Every displayed identity holds. Sections
1-2 provide witnesses for all expanded arithmetic and bit predicates.
This proves the compiler's soundness and completeness for arbitrary
run length, register values, and number of input registers.

4. Boolean compilation and hierarchical substitution

For integer-valued polynomials, a sum of squares vanishes exactly
when every summand vanishes, and a product vanishes exactly when
at least one factor vanishes. Induction over the formula DAG proves
Builder.polynomial: equations become differences, conjunctions
become sums of squares, and disjunctions become products.
Empty conjunction/disjunction give 0/1 respectively. Fresh existential
variables can be pulled out across either connective because N is
nonempty; unused-branch witnesses can be assigned zero.

Node hash-consing identifies only literally identical expressions.
Every variable receives a fresh index, so it cannot merge distinct
witnesses. Topological node order gives the usual polynomial semantics.

HierarchicalBuilder replaces a power formula by the same fixed power
polynomial, substituting its three arguments and a fresh block of 79
witnesses. These blocks are allocated consecutively and do not overlap
ordinary variables or other blocks. Induction on construction steps
therefore gives exactly the flat compiler's expression after literal
substitution, with the same variable numbering. The earlier theorem
applies unchanged. The call's value is a polynomial value; it is not
an additional assertion that every power subcall vanishes independently
of the surrounding Boolean formula.

5. Correctness of the fixed interpreter

The numbering uniquely writes e+1=2^w(2D+1). If w=0 it halts;
otherwise B=2^w and state i uses the four base-B digits of D at
positions 4i through 4i+3. Opcode 1 increments its chosen register;
opcode 2 decrements and branches, or takes the zero branch. Other
opcodes halt. Missing digits are zero. Every finite register program
has such a code by choosing w large enough for all fields.

The primitive assembler implements preserving tests by decrementing
and restoring a positive value. copy and addInto use a private counter
to restore the consumed operand. ifLt compares private copies.
divmod repeatedly subtracts its positive divisor from a private
remainder, maintaining x=q*y+r; it terminates with r<y. divmod2
consumes pairs and returns the parity. All scratch variables are
initialized on entry, and the reserved jump register stays zero.

The simulated tape uses nil=0 and cons(h,t)=2^h(2t+1). uncons first
copies its input, then repeatedly halves a positive even value until
it is odd. This strictly decreases that value. It returns h,t; nil
returns zeros. cons copies both inputs before its final output write,
so the aliases used by the interpreter are safe. getList follows a
finite number of links. setList saves the traversed prefix in reverse,
replaces the selected entry, then restores the prefix. The saved list
strictly decreases during restoration. Reading past nil gives zero;
writing past nil pads zero entries.

At the main-loop entry, D,B are fixed, pc is the simulated state,
and tape encodes its registers. Repeated division extracts exactly the
four specified digits. The opcode test admits precisely 1 and 2;
the subsequent list update and pc assignment implement their stated
semantics. All operations for one simulated step terminate. Induction
on simulated steps proves the invariant. The only exit is a halt
opcode or w=0, followed by finite cleanup of all 204 registers.
Thus the 1,399-state primitive interpreter accepts e,n exactly when
the numbered program halts.

These machines are universal: two base-encoded stacks and finite
control represent a Turing tape. Push/pop use multiplication, addition,
quotient and remainder by a fixed alphabet base, all implemented by
finite increment/decrement loops. A Turing step therefore has a finite
register-program implementation, yielding an effective translation.

6. Binding to the delivered natural polynomial

integer-stage-binding-checks.json binds the compiler, hierarchical
builder, interpreter source, saved primitive program, and saved H by
SHA256. A fresh generation of the interpreter matched the saved
primitive program. Fresh compilation matched every field of the saved
natural polynomial after JSON parsing, including variable ranges,
template bodies, metadata, and root. Its structure check found
1,305,804 total variables, 293,568 parent nodes, 15,845 calls, and
one 474-node power template. This binds the preceding argument to H.

alpha-power-checks.json separately records six complete alpha witnesses
and seven complete power witnesses evaluated against the actual emitted
polynomials. They include n=0, 0^0, positive powers of zero, and
positive bases. Changing the claimed result made all thirteen evaluated
polynomials nonzero. These are transcription checks, not substitutes
for the general proofs in sections 1-5.

Conclusion

H is a universal Diophantine polynomial with natural witnesses for the
specified ordinary integer parameters. This closes the natural compiler
and interpreter obligation. To obtain the requested rational-witness U,
the exact elliptic model and certificate, the accuracy program, and the
semantics of their rational composition still must be justified.
