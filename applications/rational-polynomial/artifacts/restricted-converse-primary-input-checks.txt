Restricted pointwise converse: two primary-source checks
3 November 2026

Scope

These checks concern the applicability of two cited external results.
They do not establish the restricted converse, the multiplication
certificate, or the universality of the generated polynomial. The
OpenAI source examined is pinned to revision
4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae.

1. Kato: rational divisibility at coefficient prime two

Source: K. Kato, p-adic Hodge theory and values of zeta functions of
modular forms, Asterisque 295 (2004), pp. 117-290.
https://www.numdam.org/article/AST_2004__295__117_0.pdf
Checked printed pp. 219-223, PDF pages 104-108.

Section 12 permits p=2 and explicitly describes its two sign components.
Theorem 12.5(3) applies at height-one primes not containing p. Its bound
includes the local H^2 term. No p!=2 or full residual-image hypothesis
is imposed in that part. Theorem 12.5(4), in contrast, assumes p!=2
and the stated SL_2 image condition. It cannot supply the desired
integral estimate at two. Remark 12.8.2 gives the non-CM open-subgroup
image statement at every coefficient place.

Consequence for the audit: the rational divisibility invocation in
the cyclotomic source has the correct prime range. Its separate
fixed-form construction must still justify the uniform integral
bound before taking quadratic characters. The local H^2 correction
must be retained or absorbed with proof; it cannot be discarded just
because the desired representation is attached to an elliptic curve.
This check does not validate those additional steps by citation alone.

2. Waldspurger comparison as stated by Purkait

Source: S. Purkait, Explicit application of Waldspurger's theorem.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1208.4329
Checked pp. 6-7: Theorems 5-7 and Corollary 5.2, including footnote 3.

Corollary 5.2 requires (H1), membership in the specified half-integral
weight eigenspace, divisibility of N/2 by the integral-weight level,
and that the two squarefree indices have square ratio locally at
every prime dividing N. It does not impose (H2). Footnote 3 allows
any form in that eigenspace. The identity uses coefficient squares,
not absolute-value squares.

Theorem 5 makes (H1) necessary for a nonzero such eigenspace. Thus
the source's co:even argument can discharge (H1) after establishing
its nonzero scalar-character form. Trivial character of the original
elliptic newform alone is not a justification for arbitrary subsequent
nonquadratic character changes. The support-local vector construction
and its resulting character and level remain essential dependencies.

Once those hypotheses hold, the common quadratic identity on the
whole eigenspace implies proportional coefficient functionals:
over C, (ell_D-c ell_0)(ell_D+c ell_0)=0 in its polynomial ring
forces one factor to vanish identically. This includes oldvectors.

Status consequence

Neither check found a prime-range or (H2) obstruction to the cited
uses. Both checks leave the source's constructive and uniformity
arguments to be proved. In particular, they do not promote the
current candidate to an unconditional universal polynomial.

The ZFC specialization remains a conditional consequence of that
target: if U(e,n,w) represents halting for ordinary integer inputs,
the code e_ZFC of a search for a finite ZFC contradiction proof gives
P(w)=U(e_ZFC,0,w), with a rational solution precisely when a finite
ZFC contradiction proof exists. This is a multivariable polynomial.
