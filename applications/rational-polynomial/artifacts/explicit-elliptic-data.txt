Explicit elliptic data for the finite certificate
2 November 2026

This note supplies two numerical choices and polynomial formulas for the
conditional universal-equation construction. It does not settle its
pointwise-converse dependency. The subsequent exact computation and
height argument in elliptic-generator-proof.txt now identify a generator:
P=((1641-1160sqrt(2))/8,(-123720+87483sqrt(2))/32) generates 2E(F).

1. The fixed curve and its rank
-------------------------------
Let s=sqrt(2), d=75-53s and
  E: y^2=x^3-d^2 x.
Then d^2=11243-7950s and Norm(d)=7. The rank computation in the pinned
source is a complete two-isogeny descent:

* O_F=Z[s] is a PID by the Minkowski bound sqrt(2)<2.
* Unit squareclasses are distinguished by their two real signs.
* For alpha on E, valuation parity limits its image to the eight
  squareclasses generated by -1, 1+s, and d.
* The four two-torsion classes are 1,-1,d,-d. The point
    Q=(-d w,2s d^2), w=-5+4s,
  supplies a class with opposite real signs and hence the whole image
  has order eight. Direct substitution uses w^3-w=-8d.
* On the isogenous curve E':y^2=x^3+4d^2x, every nonzero x is totally
  positive. Its valuations are even outside 2d. At the place above 2,
  the unequal valuations 3v(x),4+v(x) exclude odd v(x). At d, only
  v(x)=1 could remain, but this would require a root of u^2=-4 in F_7,
  which does not exist.
  Thus x is a square and im(alpha')={1}.

The two-isogeny index identity gives
  8*1 = 2^(rank E(F)+2),
so rank E(F)=1.

2. The torsion-killing multiplier is 2
--------------------------------------
At the place (17,s-6), the reduction has d=12. At (23,s-5), it has d=17.
These are good places since the discriminant is 64d^6.

Here is an exhaustive count of the affine x fibers; an x in the middle
column has two y values. All unlisted x have none.

 p   x with y=0        x with two y values
17   0,5,12           2,4,6,7,10,11,13,15
23   0,6,17           4,12,13,14,15,16,18,20,21,22

Including O gives orders 20 and 24.

Reduction is injective on torsion of order prime to the residue
characteristic. For each prime other than 17,23, its torsion order
therefore divides gcd(20,24)=4. The 17-primary part is zero by reduction
at 23, and the 23-primary part is zero by reduction at 17. Thus the
whole torsion group has order at most four. The points
  O,(0,0),(d,0),(-d,0)
already supply four. Consequently
  E(F)_tors = E(F)[2] = (Z/2Z)^2.

One can therefore set m=2 in the model carrier
  D=2E(F)=Z P
for some generator P. This torsion calculation alone does not prove that
2Q is a generator. The separate proof in elliptic-generator-proof.txt
does prove it, using a complete small-height search.

The injection just used can also be seen directly: on the reduction
kernel the formal multiplication series for n prime to p has unit
linear coefficient n; it has no nonzero n-torsion in the maximal ideal.

3. The exact truncation at precision 25
---------------------------------------
For z=-x/y, the curve equation gives
  z^2 x^2-x-d^2 z^2=0,
and on the branch near O,
  x=(1+sqrt(1+4d^2 z^4))/(2z^2).
Substitution into dx/(2y) yields
  dx/(2y) = dz/sqrt(1+4d^2 z^4).

Therefore the formal logarithm has coefficients
  ell(z) = sum_(j>=0) (-1)^j binom(2j,j) d^(2j) z^(4j+1)/(4j+1).
Its degree-25 truncation is
  ell_25(z)
    = z -(2/5)d^2 z^5 +(2/3)d^4 z^9 -(20/13)d^6 z^13
        +(70/17)d^8 z^17 -12d^10 z^21 +(924/25)d^12 z^25.

Multiplying by 16575 clears every denominator. Put
  L(z)=16575z -6630d^2 z^5 +11050d^4 z^9 -25500d^6 z^13
        +68250d^8 z^17 -198900d^10 z^21 +612612d^12 z^25.
Every logarithm RATIO in the certificate may use L instead of ell_25.
The constant cancels, and for p>26 it is a unit.

The adjacent file formal-logarithm-25.json gives the seven coefficient
pairs a+b sqrt(2) as exact integers. Their generation used only integer
arithmetic and the multiplication rule
  (a,b)(c,e)=(ac+2be,ae+bc).
As an independent algebraic check, the derivative coefficients
1,-2,6,-20,70,-252,924 satisfy
  (1+4T)(1-2T+6T^2-20T^3+70T^4-252T^5+924T^6)^2
       =1 mod T^7.
This verifies the required invariant differential through degree 24.

For p>26, the usual integral invariant-differential coefficient estimate
v(c_t)>=-v_p(t) gives
  v(ell(z)-ell_25(z)) >= v(z)+25
whenever v(z)>=1. Together with the exact logarithm homomorphism, this
is the precision required by the certificate.

4. Rational-coordinate formulas for the carrier
------------------------------------------------
Write x=x0+x1 s and y=y0+y1 s. The affine curve equations over Q are
  y0^2+2y1^2 = x0^3+6x0 x1^2-11243x0+15900x1,
  2y0 y1 = 3x0^2 x1+2x1^3-11243x1+7950x0.

Use a five-coordinate code (o,x0,x1,y0,y1), with o=1 for O and o=0
for affine points; the four unused coordinates at O are zero.
Thus each group point has a unique code.

Membership in D is existential without invoking a generator:
  either the code is O,
  or there exist X,Y,H in F and u in Q with
    Y^2=X^3-d^2 X,
    u Norm(Y)=1,
    2Y H=3X^2-d^2,
    x=H^2-2X,
    y=H(X-x)-Y.
The affine branch has o=0. Here Norm(Y)=Y0^2-2Y1^2, and each F-equation
means its two rational component equations. These are exactly the
doubling formulas for a non-two-torsion preimage. Every nonzero point
of 2E(F) has such a preimage, and O is already covered separately.

All coefficients in this predicate are explicit integers. Conjunctions
can be encoded by sums of squares over Q, and the two branches by a
product of their respective sum-of-squares polynomials. No integrality
predicate is needed for any of these rational witness coordinates.

5. A useful smaller isomorphic equation
---------------------------------------
With epsilon=1+s, one has d epsilon^4=3-s. The change
  x'=epsilon^4 x, y'=epsilon^6 y
gives
  y'^2=x'^3-(3-s)^2 x'.
On this curve R=(1+s,2s) is nontorsion, since substitution gives y'^2=8
and the torsion calculation above leaves only points with y'=0.
This is the smaller equation used in elliptic-generator-proof.txt.
The point substitution alone does not prove primitivity; that proof
additionally uses a height bound and an exhaustive exact search.
