ORDINARY SPLIT WITNESS FOR THE POLE-PREDICATE FAMILY

For E_l:y^2=x(x-l)(x+3l), with positive squarefree l=11 modulo 12,
the ordinary coefficient test already has the
nonempty split witness required in the final argument. The subsequent
extra division by four in the general split-witness proof is not needed.
The dependencies retained here are the exact even coefficient comparison,
the least-depth normalization, and the integral trace interface audited
in coefficient-trace-audit.txt. This conclusion alone does not prove
the pointwise converse or the universal equation.

1. The ordinary test and its extra-depth alternative
----------------------------------------------------
Fix a negative minimum-depth even unit k, with fresh ramification outside
the old support, and put K=Q(sqrt(k)). Choose the coefficient form for
E_l^(k), normalized by a(1)=1. The exact coefficient comparison gives

                   v_2(a(D)) >= omega(D)

for the allowed squarefree positive D. Full rational two-torsion makes
the weight omega(D). This is an exact comparison within one local filter,
not a subtraction of two estimates with unknown error constants.

Every nonzero a(D) is a rational number times a root of unity, so its
normalized 2-adic valuation is an integer. Suppose no nonempty product
of K-split primes attains equality. Then

        v_2(a(D)) >= omega(D)+1                         (1)

for every nonempty allowed all-K-split D, with the convention v(0)=infinity.

Write eta for the phase character and psi for the nebentypus.
Here psi/eta=chi_(-4), and eta is odd. At an allowed K-split prime p,
let a=a_p(E_l^(k)) and set

 B_p = (U_p-eta(p)V_p)g/2,
 H_p = (B_p - B_p[1]Theta_1 - B_p[p]Theta_p)/2,
 Theta_J = sum_m eta(m)m q^(J*m^2).

All fixed support sieves are retained. The coefficient/trace audit
establishes that H_p is integral. Multiplication by the integral
weight-1/2 theta series, congruent to 1 modulo 2, allows the weight-two
Galois trace construction without changing reduced coefficients.

2. The Frobenius trace computation
----------------------------------
Choose a Frobenius lift phi_p at p that lies in G_K. On a conductor-one
ramified principal-series constituent let the unramified eigenvalue be
lambda, so U_p=lambda. If mu is the second Frobenius eigenvalue, then

 U_p^2 t(phi_p)=lambda^2(lambda+mu)
              =U_p^3+d(phi_p)U_p.

On the unraised unary constituent the corresponding equality is the
ordinary good-prime coefficient identity. Thus on the expression at issue,

       (t(phi_p)H_p)[p^2]
             =H_p[p^3]+(d(phi_p)H_p)[p].              (2)

The use of a Frobenius lift at a ramified place causes no ambiguity:
the principal-series formula is an identity with the determinant of
that same lift.

Replace t(phi_p), to the finite precision required, by the trace at a
fresh prime p' with the same finite Galois data. Include every fixed
rational quadratic support character in that data. Then p' is split
in K and has the same support unit classes as p, so D=pp' belongs to
the total square filter even if p by itself does not.

At the left of (2), the good p' coefficient rule gives H_p[p^2 p'].
The unary terms have no such coefficient. Up to the phase eta(p), the
remaining coefficient is

                   (a-1) a(pp')/4.

By (1), v_2(a(pp'))>=3; this coefficient is therefore zero modulo 2.
This calculation explains why no individual filter condition on p
is needed: only the matched product pp' is used in (1).

The determinant term at index p on the right of (2) vanishes. Indeed,
B_p and the Theta_p summand have the same determinant character and
their coefficients cancel at p; Theta_1 has no coefficient at p.

It remains to compute H_p[p^3]. The phase-free coefficients at
1,p^2,p^4 are respectively

                        1, a-1, a(a-1)-p.

Hence, removing the common unit eta(p)^2,

 H_p[p^3]
   = [a^2-2a-p+1-p(a-2)]/4
   = (a-1)(a-p-1)/4.                                  (3)

Equations (1)-(2) make (3) zero modulo 2. Full rational two-torsion
implies a is even, so a-1 is odd. The no-witness assumption therefore
forces

                 a_p(E_l^(k)) = p+1 modulo 8          (4)

at every allowed good K-split p.

3. An elementary contradiction for this family
----------------------------------------------
Take a good prime p not dividing 6l with p=1 modulo 4 and (l/p)=-1.
For a curve with all three roots in F_p, a nonzero two-torsion point
at root r can be twice a rational point only if both root differences
r-r' are squares.

For roots 0,l,-3l:
* at 0 the difference -l is a nonsquare;
* at l the difference l is a nonsquare;
* at -3l the difference -4l is a nonsquare.

Thus no nonzero two-torsion point is divisible by 2 in E_l(F_p).
A finite abelian 2-group containing E_l[2] but having order greater
than four would contain an element of order four; its double would
contradict this conclusion. Therefore

          E_l(F_p)[2^infinity]=(Z/2Z)^2,
          #E_l(F_p)=4 modulo 8,
          a_p(E_l)=p+1-4 modulo 8.                    (5)

Because k has a fresh ramified prime outside the support of Q(i,sqrt(l)),
K is independent of that biquadratic field. Chebotarev supplies p,
outside any specified finite exceptional set, with

                    p=1 mod 4, (k/p)=1, (l/p)=-1.

At these primes E_l^(k) and E_l have the same point count. Thus (5)
contradicts (4). The ordinary test must have a nonempty K-split unit.

This argument needs the chosen negative companion to have fresh
ramification, which follows by excluding the old support when choosing
its unit. It does not apply to an arbitrary predetermined K.
It also needs l to be a nonsquare, as is guaranteed by the stated range.
The argument using (l/p)=-1 does not apply to l=1.

4. Orientation correction
-------------------------
Let V0 be the finite elementary label space for K, unramified outside
the conjugation-stable fixed support S0. Let c be complex conjugation.
Since K is imaginary, an order-two complex conjugation provides

                    G_Q = G_K semidirect <c>.

A c-invariant quadratic character of G_K extends to G_Q by assigning
c value zero. At a rational prime outside S0, inertia lies inside G_K,
since S0 contains the ramification of K; the extension remains
unramified there.

If a configuration has rational total zero on all rational quadratic
characters unramified outside S0, its oriented total v annihilates
the invariant dual space (V0*)^c. Linear algebra gives

       annihilator(ker(1+c*)) = image(1+c),
                    so v belongs to (1+c)V0.

Flipping a vertex with label w changes the total by (1+c)w.
A reserved supply of labels can therefore make any prescribed change
in this image, without changing a rational label. The desired change
between two totals lies in the same image.

These flips can be read as conjugation choices along a contraction
forest, as permitted by gr:realization. They need not change the
terminal configuration separately at different addresses.

5. Minimum seeds and compatible packet lifts
--------------------------------------------
Let r be the least positive count of a split unit. For r>=2, a finite
difference in an edge contracts to r-1 nonempty vertices and vanishes
by minimality. For r=1 there is no edge. The unit at this count therefore
depends only on its individual labels. This remains valid if the empty
configuration is also a unit; no contraction to the empty graph is used.

For the final common packet, an arbitrary pair of projected even and
odd labels need not have a simultaneous lift. It suffices to choose
compatible lifts of each required even label and each required odd
label separately, and take their union. Fresh ramification of K ensures
that every old odd label can be realized at a K-split prime. Duplicate
the full labels so the packet has rational total zero, and reserve
enough labels for orientation correction.

For the even contraction, retain r-1 anchors, adjust the total within
(1+c)V0, and merge all remaining vertices into a nonempty last cluster.
Its label is forced to be the final seed label. For the odd contraction,
rational total zero similarly fixes the last cluster. These are separate
certificates showing the two terminal polynomials are nonzero.
The two-symbol address theorem, not the certificates alone, makes both
tests units at every nonzero address.

6. The CM branch is unnecessary here
------------------------------------
For y^2=x^3+2l*x^2-3l^2*x one has

       c4=208 l^2, Delta=2304 l^6,
       j=c4^3/Delta=35152/9.

This rational number is not an integer. A CM j-invariant is an
algebraic integer, so E_l is non-CM, as is every quadratic twist.
Thus the restricted cyclotomic argument uses the non-CM rational
divisibility theorem of Kato. Its separate CM substitute is not a
dependency for this pole-predicate family.

This does not remove the CM case of the five-contact height estimate:
that estimate concerns a different varying family of abelian surfaces.

Conclusion and remaining dependencies
--------------------------------------
The ordinary nonempty split witness and its orientation correction
are established relative to the coefficient/trace interface already
audited. The general deeper divided-form branch is not needed for
this family. The cyclotomic comparison is supplied in cyclotomic-noncm-audit.txt,
and the odd lower bound, height comparison and final transfer are
assembled in restricted-pointwise-assembly.txt. Matching the exact
descent and pole-certificate hypotheses remains necessary before
asserting the whole certificate's completeness.
