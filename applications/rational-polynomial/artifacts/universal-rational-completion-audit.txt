Completion audit of the universal rational polynomial
3 November 2026

Objective and evidence standard

The objective is one fixed polynomial U with integer coefficients such
that, for ordinary nonnegative integer parameters e,n, existence of a
rational witness tuple is equivalent to program e halting on input n.
The program numbering must be effective and universal. A computable
replacement of e,n by externally calculated elliptic coordinates would
not meet the requested interface.

The candidate audited here is universal-rational-equation.json:
 SHA256 be7347c439459759121cda9656eda501afd75ca48897aa0f0acf5f85f6a43b1f
 40,644,116 bytes; 878,664,928,321 rational witnesses.

The mathematical evidence is the written argument in
universal-rational-end-to-end-proof.txt and the component arguments
listed below. The computation evidence checks transcription, finite
algebra, allocation and source/artifact identity. Passing those checks
does not establish the mathematical theorems. This audit makes no
claim of proof-assistant verification or independent expert review.

Requirements and dispositions

R1. One ordinary polynomial, with fixed integer coefficients.
Disposition: established by the finite expression semantics and the
termination argument, not by a finite sample of numerical evaluations.
End-to-end sections 1-2 check acyclic substitution, sum/product Boolean
encoding, denominator clearing, fixed division-polynomial recurrences,
and the finite false-triple guard. The catalogue search terminates by
Dirichlet; the local multiplier search terminates by the finite-index
formal-subgroup argument. The dyadic argument explicitly allows for
division by 2 and sqrt(2) when extracting rational coefficients.
All choices precede e,n. No integrality or halting oracle occurs in U.
The coefficients are specified by finite algorithms, not printed as
expanded decimal integers or an expanded monomial list.

R2. An exact, explicitly identified cyclic carrier.
Disposition: established by explicit-elliptic-data.txt and
elliptic-generator-proof.txt. The elementary descent gives rank one;
the torsion calculation and exhaustive height-bounded search identify
the generator of 2E(Q(sqrt(2))). The search bounds are proved before
enumeration. verify-elliptic-generator.js checks every candidate in
that bound, rather than sampling multiples of the proposed generator.

R3. Exact index multiplication for the actual saved template.
Disposition: established by multiplication-certificate-correctness-
proof.txt using the inputs R2, R4, R5 and R6 below.
Its soundness is proved for arbitrary rational assignments: membership
constraints first put the three inputs in the cyclic carrier; the
common-group valuation argument gives all twelve tests at one place;
the two anchors give congruence of the root indices modulo p^25; the
height bound confines false triples to the fixed guard's box.
Its completeness constructs every required group, normalization and
pole witness for each true triple. Constants, contact set, all 27
slopes, twelve tests and six groups agree with the saved template.

R4. An applicable height estimate with the actual fixed constant.
Disposition: established by six-contact-height-proof.txt, with
six-contact-height-model.txt, explicit-noncm-height-bound.txt and
effective-cm-height-bound.txt. The proof covers all allowed rational
arguments, including negative valuations. It bounds the entire fine
cover, its components and its Hom field, treats CM and non-CM fibers,
and gives the explicit theta comparison and exponent 24.
height-primary-input-review.txt checks the stated published inputs.
The exact symbolic constant definitions are bound to U by
height-constant-binding-checks.json. Finite identities and quotient
calculations are checked separately by verify-six-contact.js.
This is the SIX-contact replacement. It is not a completed validation
of the original five-contact theta comparison.

R5. The prime patterns needed for completeness.
Disposition: established by twenty-two-prime-pattern-proof.txt from
the cited Green--Tao--Ziegler theorem and the displayed elementary
sieve. Its parameter A can be any integer. The small-prime residues,
fixed catalogue, lower count and upper bad-prime count use compatible
orders of limits: first fix the auxiliary cutoff, then increase the
counting scale. Only gcd(d1,d2)=4 at each individual selected prime
is required. No assertion about different primes' reduction orders,
or unproved simultaneous quadratic prime-pattern assertion, is used.

R6. Completeness of the exact eleven-witness pole predicate.
Disposition: established by restricted-converse-and-pole-completeness-
proof.txt and pole-descent-completeness-audit.txt, using the restricted
pointwise argument and component proofs identified below.
The two descent parameters are positive squarefree and 11 modulo 12.
This supplies the nonsquareness used in the ordinary split-witness
argument. The finite Selmer dimension is converted to full Selmer
corank one using the Cassels--Tate pairing; it is not directly equated
with positive Mordell--Weil rank. The resulting rational points give
the literal guarded equations in explicit-pole-predicate.txt.
The elementary opposite direction, exclusion of negative odd poles,
is independent of this completeness argument.

R6a. No circular arithmetic or quantifier dependency inside R6.
Disposition: checked in restricted-pointwise-assembly.txt,
restricted-converse-and-pole-completeness-proof.txt and
paired-functional-and-uniformity-proof.txt. The dependency order is:
 nonvanishing and the even cyclotomic bound;
 even coefficient comparison and even symbols;
 finite Heegner classes, limit interface and determinant switch;
 varying-companion bound and unnormalized height comparison;
 odd arithmetic lower bound, then odd minimum and symbols;
 a fixed companion, two finite address tests, then binary transfer.
The varying-companion constant depends on a prime-factor bound, not on
the particular companion. The later clearing constant may depend on
the one companion that has already been fixed. Precision and binary
dimension precede the choice of the actual coefficient stage.
The complete supporting files are included in the hash manifest.
Kato and Purkait range checks are recorded in
restricted-converse-primary-input-checks.txt.

R6b. Correction to the intermediate matrix-inverse argument.
Disposition: repaired. The earlier claim of a precision-independent
pole bound for an actual inverse was false. With
 det A_i=t^d_i v_i+2w_i and d_i<=4,
use C_i=adj(A_i)/(t^d_i v_i), not A_i^{-1}. Then BC=1+2F,
and C and F have poles at most four. Modulo 2^M the inverse is
 C sum_(j=0)^(M-1) (-2F)^j,
with pole bound 4M. The required clearing bound remains 16mM.
determinant-transfer-audit.txt already contained this argument;
paired-functional-and-uniformity-proof.txt now uses it consistently.
verify-determinant-schur.js checks 72 finite cases and records the
counterexample (t+2)^{-1} modulo 8 to the erroneous stronger claim.
Those finite cases support, but do not replace, the geometric-series
proof. No coefficient or variable count in U changed.

R7. An effective universal program numbering and natural polynomial.
Disposition: established by integer-halting-correctness-proof.txt,
integer-compiler-spec.txt and universal-interpreter-spec.txt.
The proof covers the packed-history and no-carry invariants, zero-time
runs, unbounded register values, the cited alpha/power formulas and
the universal interpreter. integer-stage-binding-checks.json checks
the full freshly compiled natural circuit and interpreter, not merely
their dimensions. The alpha/power evaluations are finite supplementary
checks, not the proof of universality.

R8. A total accuracy predicate and exact rational interpretation.
Disposition: established by accuracy-correctness-proof.txt and
rational-universality-conditional-proof.txt, whose hypothesis M is
supplied by R3. The accuracy proof checks the signed arithmetic,
pair decoding, Jacobian exceptional cases, primitive lowering and
specialization of the universal natural polynomial. The interpretation
preserves signed intermediate values. Each private power block is
used once and evaluates a polynomial value; it is not forced to zero
inside an unselected branch. Source/artifact binding and private scopes
are checked by the corresponding accuracy and translation reports.

R9. The user's ordinary integer parameter interface, in both directions.
Disposition: established in end-to-end sections 7-8. The accuracy
predicate ensures that its trace ratio is within 1/8 of an internal
natural index k. The bridge requires the same ratio within 1/8 of the
external integer a. Thus |a-k|<1/4 and a=k. Recurrence in the product
of the two real elliptic groups supplies a suitable positive index h
for each k, proving completeness. Recurrence is a proof of existence
of finite witnesses, not an operation in the polynomial.
No behavior on nonintegral rational input parameters is asserted.

R10. One fixed witness count and a bound to the delivered bytes.
Disposition: established by explicit allocation and the assembly audit.
 Halting recipe: 292887770443 witnesses.
 Each input bridge: 292888578934 witnesses.
 Outer elliptic coordinates: 10 witnesses.
 Total: 878664928321 witnesses, or 878664928323 variables with e,n.
Every witness range is disjoint and completely covered. The saved
polynomial regenerates byte for byte. The auditor rejects nine
deliberate mutations of essential constraints and wiring.

Conclusion and limits

R1-R10 supply the requested construction and its written mathematical
proof. The hash manifest identifies the exact proof package and its
computational evidence. Its integrity verifier does not read natural
language proofs or certify their logical validity.

This completes the construction at the level of a written proof using
the cited mathematical inputs, including the audited restricted
pointwise argument. It is not a claim that those arguments have been
accepted by the mathematical community or checked by Lean. The
original five-contact comparison remains outside the proved route.
The numerical code of a separate ZFC proof-search program has not been
compiled. Its possible specialization is a consequence of universality,
not an additional prerequisite for the polynomial constructed here.

Earlier status sentences retained in component notes describe their
then-missing premises. The dispositions above identify the subsequent
proofs supplying each premise. Metadata in U does not define additional
equations and is retained unchanged to preserve the audited file hash.
