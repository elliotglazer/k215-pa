Finite-level Heegner descent and the switch at 2
2 November 2026

Scope
-----
This note checks the finite-level arithmetic in rd:derivative of the
frozen OpenAI pointwise 2-converse. The conclusions use the classical
Heegner norm and reduction relations and Bogomolov's scalar theorem.
The argument below supplies the integral descent, the local Kummer
condition, and the finite/singular calculation at 2. It does not
establish the complete pointwise converse or the later weighted-theta
construction.

The important uniformity is that one fixed integer L suffices. Its
2-adic valuation does not grow with the number of conductor or derivative
primes. Precision losses are handled by working at a higher torsion
level, not by repeatedly multiplying the class by 2.

Notation
--------
E/Q is fixed, T=T_2(E), and K is an imaginary quadratic field satisfying
the source's split Heegner hypotheses, with K different from Q(i) and
Q(sqrt(-3)). H_f denotes its ring-class field of conductor f.
At a working level a, coefficients E[2^a] are identified with T/2^a T.
Reduction to level m<=a corresponds on torsion points to multiplication
by 2^(a-m).

For an inert good derivative prime ell, sigma is an oriented generator
of Gal(H_{f ell}/H_f), of order ell+1, and
  D_ell=sum_{i=1}^ell i sigma^i.
The lower and upper Heegner points, after any other commuting derivative
operators, will be denoted X and Y. We use the point identities
  Tr(Y)=a_ell X,        red(Y)=F red(X),                 (1)
where F is rational Frobenius on the reduced elliptic curve. Also
  F^2-a_ell F+ell=0.                                    (2)
Any fixed cuspidal correction in (1) can be removed by one fixed
multiple of the parametrization.

1. A torsion bound uniform in all ring-class conductors
-------------------------------------------------------
Bogomolov's theorem supplies g in G_Q acting on T by u times the identity,
with u in 1+4Z_2 and u!=1. Fix a complex conjugation tau and put
  z=g^2 tau g^2 tau^-1.
The square g^2 belongs to every quadratic subgroup, hence to G_K.
Conjugation by tau inverts Gal(H_f/K), so z acts trivially on every H_f.
Its Tate action is the scalar u^4.

Since 1+4Z_2 is torsion-free, u^4-1 is nonzero. Put
  e=v_2(u^4-1).
If P is a 2-primary torsion point rational over any H_f, then
  (u^4-1)P=(z-1)P=0.
The unit part of u^4-1 is invertible on a finite 2-primary group, so
  2^e P=0.                                             (3)

The same g, z and e work as K and f vary through these ring-class data.
Thus neither the number of conductor primes nor their residue degrees
enters (3).

The scalar input is stated in Bogomolov's primary publication:
https://www.mathnet.ru/php/archive.phtml?jrnid=im&option_lang=eng&paperid=1843&wshow=paper

2. Descent once from the complete conductor
-------------------------------------------
Direct multiplication in the cyclic group ring gives
  (sigma-1)D_ell=(ell+1)-sum_{i=0}^ell sigma^i.          (4)
For a product n of distinct derivative primes, the relative class
groups form a product. The ring-class exact sequence gives the local
factors F_{ell^2}^*/F_ell^*, and the only global units, 1 and -1,
have trivial images in these quotients. In particular the D_ell commute.

Assume 2^a divides ell+1 and a_ell for each ell|n. Equations (1) and
(4) make the Kummer class of D_n y_{fn} invariant under
Gal(H_{fn}/H_f). Let H=G_{H_{fn}}, G_0=G_{H_f}, and Q=G_0/H.
The relevant inflation-restriction sequence is
  H^1(Q,M^H) -> H^1(G_0,M) -> H^1(H,M)^Q -> H^2(Q,M^H),
where M=E[2^a].
By (3), M^H is killed by 2^e; therefore both outside groups are killed
by 2^e as well.

For any invariant class c, choose a lift b of 2^e c and define
  descent(c)=2^e b.
This is independent of b, because the ambiguity is killed by 2^e.
It is additive and natural under compatible coefficient maps, by the
same uniqueness argument. Its restriction is 2^(2e)c.

This operation is applied to the full group Q in one step. It is not
iterated one prime at a time. Thus its multiplier is 2^(2e), independently
of the number of factors of n. It handles nonzero rational two-torsion;
no residual irreducibility or absence of torsion is assumed.

3. The Kummer condition at the fixed bad primes
----------------------------------------------
The conductor extensions just used are unramified at the primes over 2N.
For a finite unramified local extension L'/F and the identity component
E_0(L') of the Neron model,
  H^1(Gal(L'/F),E_0(L'))=0.                             (5)
Here is the local argument. On the connected special fiber, Lang's
theorem makes the norm surjective and gives vanishing of cyclic H^1.
On each successive formal-group quotient the statement is additive
unramified descent: a normal integral basis gives zero Tate cohomology.
Induction gives the assertion for finite formal quotients. Solutions
lift compatibly by surjectivity at each quotient, or by compactness;
passing to the complete formal group proves (5).

The component group is finite. Therefore H^1(Gal(L'/F),E(L')) is killed
by its geometric component-group order. A local class that becomes
Kummer over L' has image in H^1(F,E) restricting to zero over L';
inflation-restriction and the preceding observation show that this
image is killed by that same component-group order.

Only finitely many local quadratic twisting types occur at the fixed
primes 2N. Multiply the descended class by the product of their
geometric component-group orders. This puts it in the Kummer image at
every such prime, uniformly in the unramified extensions. No ordinary
or semistable reduction hypothesis is needed.

At a good prime outside 2, the conductor, and N, the upper Kummer class
is unramified. The extension is unramified there, so the inertia group
does not change and the descended class is unramified as well. At the
active primes and the auxiliary scalar prime, the local condition is
relaxed and imposes no further restriction.

Let L be the product of the fixed parametrization multiplier,
2^(2e), and the component-group factors just described.

4. The singular coordinate, with its torsion error accounted for
----------------------------------------------------------------
Fix ell|n and apply all derivative operators other than D_ell first.
The lower field H_f splits completely at the K-prime (ell), because
this ideal is principal and generated by the rational integer ell,
which is prime to f. Hence
  F^2 red(X)=red(X).                                    (6)

Choose Q with 2^a Q=LX. The finite coordinate of the lower Kummer class is
  f=(F^2-1)red(Q)=(a_ell F-(ell+1))red(Q).              (7)

Choose Z with 2^a Z=L D_ell Y. A cocycle b for the descended upper class
can be represented so that b(g)=(g-1)Z on the upper-field Galois group.
Put
  U=(sigma-1)Z-b(sigma).
The cocycle identity shows that U is fixed by that upper-field group.
Indeed, for g in it, compare b(g sigma) with
b(sigma (sigma^-1 g sigma)); the resulting identity is gU=U.

Furthermore,
  2^a U=L((ell+1)Y-a_ell X).
Since the displayed integers are divisible by 2^a,
  U_0=L((ell+1)/2^a)Y-L(a_ell/2^a)X
is an actual point of the upper field with the same 2^a-multiple.
Thus U-U_0 is upper-field torsion, killed by 2^e.

Inertia acts trivially on the reduced curve, so reduction of b(sigma)
is -red(U). Using (1) and 2^a Q=LX, this gives
  s=(a_ell-(ell+1)F)red(Q)+epsilon,                     (8)
where epsilon is killed by 2^e. This controls the error by rational
torsion in the upper field; it does not treat arbitrary division-point
choices as harmless.

Using (2), one checks the exact integral operator identity
  (F-a_ell)(a_ell F-(ell+1))=a_ell-(ell+1)F.
Equations (7) and (8) therefore give
  s=(F-a_ell)f+epsilon.                                (9)

At a retained level m, choose a>=m+e. Reduction from E[2^a] to E[2^m]
kills epsilon. Choose rational Frobenius congruent to the fixed Tate
action J of complex conjugation modulo 2^(a+1). Its trace and determinant
then give a_ell=0 and ell=-1 modulo 2^(a+1). Consequently (9) becomes
  s=Jf mod 2^m.                                       (10)

This choice of a and of Frobenius precision is made before Chebotarev.
It changes precision, not the fixed multiplier L.

5. The new finite coordinate and the factor of two
---------------------------------------------------
All inertia translates of Y have the same reduction. Thus
  red(D_ell Y)=ell(ell+1)/2 * F red(X).                 (11)
Require v_2(ell+1)>=a+1, as ensured above. The right side is then 2^a
times an F^2-fixed point, by (6). Since H_{f ell}/H_f is totally
ramified locally at ell, a Frobenius lift can be chosen inside the
upper-field local Galois group. On that group b is the Kummer cocycle
of L D_ell Y.

For good reduction at the odd prime ell, the formal group is uniquely
2-divisible and the unramified Kummer coordinate is determined by
E(F_{ell^2})/2^a E(F_{ell^2}). Equation (11) is zero in this quotient.
The new finite coordinate therefore vanishes:
  f(P_{I union {ell}})=0 mod 2^m.                     (12)

The extra 1 in v_2(ell+1)>=a+1 is essential: merely requiring 2^a
to divide ell+1 does not absorb the denominator 2 in (11).

Equations (10) and (12) are the desired finite-level switch. They hold
at each old derivative prime too, since the derivative operators
commute and (1) holds for every transported class-group translate.

The classical calculation is compared with Howard, section 1.7:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1202.6340v1#page=19
His global odd-prime and residual-image hypotheses are not imported.
The bounded-torsion descent above supplies the replacement needed here.

6. Weighted transfer is integral and compatible with the calculations
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Let A be a finite Artin coefficient ring, let Delta=Gal(H_f/K), and let
theta=chi_h Psi be the finite character, trivial on G_{H_f}. Identify
the induced module with T_A tensor_A A[Delta], using the diagonal
G_K-action. The map
  v tensor [delta] |-> theta(delta) v
is G_K-equivariant into T_A(theta). Indeed, applying g multiplies the
right side by theta(g) and applies g to v, exactly the target action.
Using inverse-action conventions gives the same statement after
inverting both displayed character conventions.

Composing finite-index Shapiro with this map is a finite weighted
coset sum on cochains. There is no division by |Delta|. For a universal
quadratic family replace chi_h(delta) by its group element in A[G];
the same calculation proves equivariance before any sign evaluation.

Restriction and finite corestriction commute with Kummer maps.
Locally their Mackey decomposition is a sum over transported places.
At 2N the scalar Psi is trivial; after the usual quadratic-twist
identification each local term is a Kummer class, and the A-linear
sum is still one. At a derivative prime the weights in the upper and
lower class are identical. Transport the place and Frobenius with the
coset representative; (10) and (12) then hold term by term and after
the sum.

This proves the finite-level assertions in the universal group ring,
rather than first proving them at separate sign characters.

7. The unused-prime factor
--------------------------
At an unused split conductor prime q, the classical split norm
relation gives the scalar
  e_q=a_q-z-z^-1
after weighting, where z is scalar Frobenius at one of the split
places. The two-place singular local quotient has determinant
  l_q=det(1-q^-1 z Fr_q | T) det(1-q^-1 z^-1 Fr_q | T),
up to the consistent integral-unit convention for Frobenius.

The class and its conjugate dual each acquire e_q. The inverse
determinant coordinate divides by the determinant of the acyclic
singular quotient. Therefore
  u_rel=e_q^2 u_prim/l_q.
This explains both the square and the denominator in rd:unused.
The group-ring correction of determinant-transfer-audit.txt then
removes these factors integrally. Its purely algebraic part has
already been proved there.

This assertion about the paired coordinate presupposes the global
duality identification of the actual finite Selmer cones. The point
trace and local determinant calculation alone are not a construction
of that global identification.

8. Retaining actual finite witnesses
------------------------------------
After weighted transfer, each class is on the fixed target field K.
An equality of its local cohomology classes means precisely that the
difference of representative cocycles is a coboundary. Choose a
degree-zero local cochain witnessing that equality. Likewise, membership
in a local Kummer condition gives a local Kummer representative and a
boundary comparing its image to the localized global cocycle. These
are exactly the entries needed to make a cycle in the Selmer cone.

Under a strong deformation retraction (i,p,h) from original target
cochains to a bounded free model, retain pz for each cycle z and pb for
each boundary witness b. If z-z'=db then pz-pz'=d(pb) exactly.
For a comparison f to another target with projection p', the difference
between p'fz and p'fi pz is the boundary of p'fhz.
Thus the arithmetic equalities above supply finite target vectors and
their actual homotopies before a limit is taken.

This step does not require retaining the large ring-class source
complex or a vector for every coset. One performs the finite sum
first and contracts the result. The existence and uniform sizes of the
target free models, and the preservation of all evaluations in the
inner and outer limits, remain the separate limit-interface assertions.

Assessment
----------
The finite-level descent and local switch of rd:derivative check out
under the stated classical inputs. The extra precision at 2 is needed
and is available; no multiplier accumulating with the number of primes
appears. The weighted transfer also exists integrally over the
universal group ring.

This closes the finite-level class-construction portion of the audit.
It does not yet certify the complete simultaneous global-duality and
limit interface, the determinant lower bound, the weighted-theta
coefficient identities, or the pointwise 2-converse. The requested
universal rational equation consequently remains unproved here.

The subsequent supplement limit-interface-audit.txt verifies the
model, character, residual-rank, evaluation, and finite-realization
parts of this interface. The later determinant-switch and
weighted-theta/binary-family arguments remain separate obligations.
