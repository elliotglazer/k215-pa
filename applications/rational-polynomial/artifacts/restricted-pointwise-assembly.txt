SEEDS, COMPANION HEIGHTS, AND RESTRICTED POINTWISE ASSEMBLY
2 November 2026

Scope and status
----------------
This note assembles the source-level argument for
E_l:y^2=x(x-l)(x+3l), l>0 squarefree, l=11 modulo 12, under the hypothesis
s_2(E_l)=1. The conclusion of the argument is analytic rank one, hence
Mordell--Weil rank one and finite Sha by the forward theorem.

The pointwise argument is read at openai/math commit
4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae:
https://github.com/openai/math/blob/4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae/preprints/A-pointwise-2-converse-for-elliptic-curves-with-rational-two-torsion-October-7-2026/build/sections/pointwise.tex

This is an assembly audit. Each nonclassical input is identified by
its separate written audit below; it is not a machine-checked proof.
The range l=11 modulo 12 is the range needed by the pole predicate.
This explicit restriction corrects the older, broader wording of this
note: its elementary ordinary split-witness proof requires l to be
a nonsquare and cannot be used at l=1. The combined proof is now in
restricted-converse-and-pole-completeness-proof.txt.
It does not certify all cases of the general pointwise theorem or
instantiate the universal rational polynomial. The residue-matrix
descent used to supply s_2=1 in the pole construction is a separate input.

In this family every good prime has t_p=2, so w(h)=omega(h).
All curves and their quadratic twists are non-CM, since j=35152/9.
Neither a quadratic residual splitting field nor the isogeny-orientation
donor argument is needed here.

1. Dependency order
-------------------
Use these results in the following order.

A. The classical modularity, parity, and forward Gross--Zagier--Kolyvagin
   results. Parity concerns the full 2-primary Selmer corank, not just
   finite 2-Selmer dimension or Mordell--Weil rank.

B. The independent analytic witnesses in nonvanishing-audit.txt and
   the non-CM cyclotomic argument in cyclotomic-noncm-audit.txt.
   These require no pointwise converse.

C. The even coefficient system, its exact value ratios, and the
   integral coefficient/trace rules in coefficient-trace-audit.txt.
   The cyclotomic lower bound supplies its minimum-depth premise.

D. Finite Heegner descent, compatible limit models, determinant switches,
   and central specialization:
   finite-heegner-descent-audit.txt,
   limit-interface-audit.txt,
   determinant-switch-audit.txt,
   central-specialization-audit.txt.
   These give the ring-class lower bound at already known simple zeros.

E. The partitionable even bound, bounded varying companion, and height
   ratio below. These establish the odd lower bound BEFORE the odd
   coefficients are normalized.

F. The weighted odd detector in weighted-theta-audit.txt; the minimum
   and trace arguments in coefficient-trace-audit.txt; the common finite
   graph and two-symbol address construction in arithmetic-graph-audit.txt.

G. A fixed companion and the ordinary nonempty split witness in
   ordinary-split-witness-audit.txt, followed by the fixed-companion
   ring-class transfer. The latter uses the algebraic clearing and
   coefficient comparison in determinant-transfer-audit.txt together
   with the arithmetic diagrams in D.

This order prevents the odd minimum from being used to prove its own
lower bound. The varying companion of E and the fixed companion of G
serve different purposes and must not be interchanged.

2. Minimum seeds and rational reserves
--------------------------------------
In the full-two-torsion case, every variable vertex is split in the
residual sense. If r is the least positive count of a unit witness,
then for r>=2 every edge derivative is a symbol on r-1 nonempty
vertices and vanishes. For r=1 there is no edge. Thus the value at
this count depends only on the individual labels, not mutual edges.
This assertion also holds if the empty configuration is a unit.

For a list with the same rational total label as an r-vertex seed,
retain r-1 seed labels as anchors and merge all other vertices into
a last nonempty cluster. Total-label equality gives the last seed
label. The contraction forest has a unit as its output, so the
original terminal polynomial has a nonzero finite difference and
is therefore nonzero.

Supply enough copies of each needed label, then duplicate every full
label. The resulting packet has zero total in the elementary label
space. Any positive union of sufficiently large such packets still
contains the required anchors and a nonempty final cluster. Repeated
labels are realized by distinct primes.

3. Partitionable even parameters
--------------------------------
Fix an even symbol and a parameter h whose terminal list contracts
to a unit seed. Suppose s_2(E^h)=0.

If h is empty, its contraction certificate already asserts that the
empty value is a unit. Otherwise keep its nonempty terminal set
stationary. The one-symbol address theorem adds primary vertices,
activated by nonzero linear forms, and makes every nonzero address
a unit. Each has nonzero central value and

                  v(L_*(h_x)) <= 2w(h_x)+C.

Its finite Sha length is nonnegative, so the same uniform constant
bounds v(L_*)-2w-s. The cyclotomic missing-vertex theorem now applies
at the stationary base and gives

              L_*(h)!=0,  v(L_*(h)) <= 2w(h)+s(h)+C'.       (1)

The dimension needed may depend on the base and the requested
precision. The valuation constant in (1) does not.

4. A bounded companion for each known odd parameter
---------------------------------------------------
Prepare the fixed positive filter, its support, and a fixed negative
genus partner h_* with nonzero central value. Consider h on that filter
already known to have analytic rank one.

Prepare an even negative-index symbol for each possible total at the
finite fixed support. There are only finitely many. A negative odd
fundamental discriminant k has |k|=3 modulo 4, so the corresponding
positive coefficient index has nonzero total type at 2. Its minimum
seed is therefore nonempty.

Insert into the prospective k' a bounded dummy supply containing
enough copies of all these seed labels. In this full-two-torsion case
there are no bad simple vertices or donor balances to correct.
Choose a fresh prime if necessary to exclude exceptional imaginary
fields. All dummy primes avoid h and the fixed support.

Absorb h and the dummy product into the curve, and apply bounded-factor
nonvanishing to finish k'. Prescribe the finishing factor by CRT so
that the final k' is negative, fundamental, prime to 2Nh, and a square
at every place of 2Nh. These prescriptions are reduced nonempty
progressions. The final twist has positive sign because

 epsilon(E^(hk'))=epsilon(E^h) chi_k'(-N_(E^h))=(-1)(-1)=+1.

The fact that the dummy product has already been absorbed does not
change the result: quadratic twisting by the finishing factor gives
exactly E^(hk'). The new factor has at most the absolute number A0
of primes established by the nonvanishing argument. Its size threshold
may depend on h and all its local conditions.

No particular final type at auxiliary fixed primes was assumed.
Select the prepared seed for the actual total. The dummy reserves
make hk' partitionable; analytic nonvanishing gives corank zero by
the forward theorem. Thus (1) yields

  omega(k')<=A,  L_*(hk')!=0,
  v(L_*(hk'))<=2w(hk')+s(hk')+C,                       (2)

with A,C independent of h.

5. The exact height factor
--------------------------
Let j_h(k) measure the index of the conductor-|h| ring-class sum
over Q(sqrt(k)), and j(P_h) the index of the conductor-one genus sum
over Q(sqrt(hh_*)). Assume the two relevant factors have analytic
orders one and zero.

Cai--Shu--Tian, Theorem 1.1, expresses the height of the unnormalized
character sum using the factor c*sqrt(|D|), with fixed modular degree
and Petersson norm:
https://msp.org/ant/2014/8-10/ant-v8-n10-p05-p.pdf
(Theorem 1.1 is on printed page 2525.)

For the first sum, restriction of chi_h to Q(sqrt(k)) has ring-class
conductor exactly |h|. At q|h the prime splits in that field and the
nontrivial ramified quadratic character has conductor q. For the
second sum the biquadratic genus character is unramified, so its
ring-class conductor is one.

Every level prime splits in both fields. Consequently the theorem's
mu(N,D) is zero. The extra-unit fields have been excluded, so the
unit factor is one. The two heights are therefore fixed nonzero
modular factors times

   |h| sqrt(|k|) L'(E^h,1)L(E^(hk),1),
   sqrt(|hh_*|) L'(E^h,1)L(E^(h_*),1),

respectively. There is no omitted product over the conductor primes.

Taking their ratio cancels the derivative. On the algebraic side,
both nonzero points project to the same rational rank-one line for
E^h; restriction and the two quadratic projections have composites
[2], so comparison of the integral free lines costs a bounded index.
The regulator cancels. On the analytic side the remaining varying
factor is sqrt(|hk|)L(E^(hk),1), up to the fixed period and h_* factor.
Thus

                  2j_h(k)-2j(P_h)=v(L_*(hk))+O(1).     (3)

The 2-adic valuation is taken after the regulator cancels and the
ratio lies in the algebraic normalized value; it is not applied to
a transcendental real height. The error is uniform for fixed k and,
in the varying-companion application, for bounded omega(k).

6. Odd lower bound before odd normalization
-------------------------------------------
The ring-class determinant lower bound at a known simple zero gives

 2j_h(k') >= 4w(h)+s(h)+s(hk')-C.

Subtract (3) and use (2), w(hk')=w(h)+w(k'), and w(k')<=A:

 2j(P_h)
   >=4w(h)+s(h)+s(hk')-v(L_*(hk'))-C
   >=2w(h)+s(h)-2w(k')-C
   >=2w(h)+s(h)-C'.

This is the lower bound required by the odd coefficient minimum.
Only even symbols and independent analytic nonvanishing have been
used to obtain it.

7. Stabilizing the odd symbol
-----------------------------
Keep the same ultrafilter used for the minimum-depth construction.
The moving detecting prime adds at most two places to a fixed
quadratic field and imposes only a quadratic residue-line test.
Thus the dimensions of the elementary label spaces are bounded.
There are finitely many addition/conjugation/projection tables for
such spaces after choosing bases.

At each fixed weight there are finitely many graphs and truth tables.
The ultrafilter selects compatible tables, giving one limit symbol.
Its minimum witness has bounded weight and therefore a fixed witness
type on a large set. If the empty value is a unit, an actual sufficiently
late detector already proves analytic rank one at the base.

Otherwise retain its nonempty minimum seed. Later enrichment by a
fixed companion is performed on the SAME ultrafilter, with compatible
projections, so it does not change the intrinsic odd symbol or minimum.
An actual stage is chosen after the dimension and finite networks,
using a finite intersection of large sets. No stage is required to
serve every dimension or every unbounded graph weight.

8. A fixed companion and two simultaneous tests
-----------------------------------------------
Choose a separate negative even unit k, avoiding the intrinsic odd
support, the required torsion ramification, and the exceptional fields.
Fix k before choosing any cube dimension. It has a fresh ramified prime,
so K=Q(sqrt(k)) is independent of the old odd label extension, including
at each actual stage whose detecting prime avoids k.

For this particular E_l, ordinary-split-witness-audit.txt proves that
the ordinary even test for E_l^(k) has a nonempty K-split witness.
The extra divided-form branch in the general paper is not needed.

One may follow the source using K-oriented labels and its orientation
correction. Here a simpler rational projection suffices: the ordinary
even test depends only on rational labels. Include the K-character
among the common rational tests and allow labels in its kernel.
Every old odd seed label has a compatible K-split lift by the fresh
ramification. Every even seed label is already realized at such a
prime and has some lift to the common refinement. Arbitrary pairs
of projected labels need not lift.

Take a union of enough compatible lifts for each seed separately,
include the needed reserves, and duplicate all full labels. Each
packet has common rational total zero. The even seed has total zero
on its positive square filter; the odd seed also does on its own
filter. By the reserve argument in section 2, every nonempty packet
union has two separately nonzero terminal polynomials.

Place a packet at every nonzero linear form on F_2^b. The two-symbol
address theorem supplies finite primary networks making BOTH tests
units at every nonzero address. All primary types are K-split and
neutral on the fixed support. No vertex is active at zero.

Choose an actual odd stage with sufficient precision for all graphs
in these networks and realize the common finite data by fresh primes.
The depth constants and the companion remain fixed as b increases.

9. Final transfer at the prescribed base
----------------------------------------
At every x!=0 the odd detector gives analytic order one for E^h,
and the even detector gives order zero for E^(hk). Their unit bounds
and (3) imply

                       2j_h(k)<=4w(h)+C.

Subtracting the nonnegative Sha lengths retains an upper bound for

                2j_h(k)-4w(h)-s(h)-s(hk).              (4)

At zero, the chosen even companion has Selmer corank zero by the
forward theorem. Restriction and the quadratic projections, after
rationalizing their [2] composites, give

                   s_2(E_l/K)=s_2(E_l)+s_2(E_l^(k))=1.

This is a corank identity; it does not assume an integral splitting
or finiteness of Sha at the unknown base.

The primitive generic Selmer rank is at most the central rank:
one at the base, and one at the already known simple-zero vertices.
There are no generic invariants; duality therefore gives the acyclic
or rank-one-in-degrees-(1,2) pattern required by the arithmetic family
clearing argument. Unused local blocks are generically acyclic.

If the base derivative vanished, the conductor-one Gross--Zagier
formula would make the base Heegner sum torsion. Its rational Kummer
class is zero. In generic rank zero the determinant coordinate is
already zero; in generic rank one the equal central rank allows
derived specialization and also gives u_0(0)=0.

The arithmetic construction and determinant-transfer-audit.txt provide
a bounded clearing factor and finite Laurent coefficient tests, with
bounds depending on E_l and this fixed K, not b. A sufficiently large
binary cube gives a nonzero address whose determinant constant agrees
with u_0(0) beyond the upper valuation bound supplied by (4). That is
a contradiction.

Therefore the product L(E_l,s)L(E_l^(k),s) has a nonzero first derivative
at 1. The second factor is nonzero there, so E_l has analytic rank one.
The forward Gross--Zagier--Kolyvagin theorem gives Mordell--Weil rank
one and finite Sha.

10. What this assembly does and does not finish
----------------------------------------------
Together with the listed audits and classical inputs, this completes
the source-level dependency chain for the restricted implication

                  s_2(E_l)=1 ==> rank E_l(Q)=1.

If the residue-matrix descent gives dim_F2 Sel_2(E_l)=3, the Kummer
sequence and alternating Cassels--Tate pairing reduce it to s_2(E_l)=1,
as explained in restricted-converse-audit.txt. The specified non-torsion
Selmer class then comes from a rational point.

Before using this as an unconditional universal equation, the exact
descent and pole-certificate hypotheses must still be matched, the
five-contact height constants made explicit, and the ordinary-input
polynomial actually instantiated. This assembly is not a Lean proof
and is not a claim to have audited the omitted general CM or quadratic-
residual branches.
